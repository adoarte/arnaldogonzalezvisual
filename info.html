Arnaldo Drés González (b. 1986, Caracas) is a Venezuelan multidisciplinary visual artist based in Hamburg. He holds a BFA in Mixed Media from the Universidad Nacional Experimental de las Artes (Caracas) and an MFA from the University of Applied Sciences and Arts (Ottersberg, Germany). Working across moving image, photography, drawing, performance and installation, his practice explores perception, bodily memory and transformation, investigating how familiar forms can shift between body, image, material and space.
